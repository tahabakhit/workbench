# Draft questions

The user cannot answer the questions themselves, so do not interview them
about the subject. Interview them only about the send:

- who receives it and what they know;
- what decision the answers feed, and what is needed back;
- the channel and any deadline.

Then draft the questionnaire:

- a two-line context the recipient needs and nothing more;
- questions most important first, one idea each, answerable without this
  session's context;
- closed options or a format for the answer where possible ("A, B, or
  other"), so replies compare.

Done when the draft is shown and the user has what to paste. Shorten before
adding: five questions answered beats twelve skimmed.
