# Rephrase

Your last message did not land. Say it again in ASD-STE100 style (Simplified
Technical English):

- the conclusion or the ask first, in one sentence;
- short sentences, everyday words, one idea per sentence;
- every term defined before it is used, or replaced by what it means; when
  the repo has a `GLOSSARY.md`, use its terms;
- an example if the idea is abstract;
- a premise the reader was missing may be added, but no new claims: same
  content, clearer.

Keep it shorter than the original.
