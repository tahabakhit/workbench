# Rephrase

Your last message did not land. Say it again, differently:

- the conclusion or the ask first, in one sentence;
- short sentences, everyday words, one idea per sentence;
- every term defined before it is used, or replaced by what it means;
- an example if the idea is abstract;
- nothing new: same content, clearer.

Keep it shorter than the original.
